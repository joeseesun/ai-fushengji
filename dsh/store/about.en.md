Start with ¥8,000 in training debt and 40 days to pay it off. Move between stops in Beijing, take gigs, trade compute, or deliver client projects. Leave time to rest before stress or exhaustion ends the run.

- Pick one of five careers. Spend two actions a day on work or rest, and trade or repay debt between actions.
- Check the daily paper and compare prices before you choose what to buy and where to go tomorrow.
- Deal with rent, meet fictional AI-industry characters and get a year-end report on how you played.

Use a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter. The game is in Simplified Chinese, runs offline, and keeps up to six saves and your scores locally. Save backups can be copied and pasted as JSON.
